# ROW HAUS — Brand Voice + Copy Deck

Single source of truth for on-page strings. Every string below is final. One option per slot.

---

## 1. Voice

- **Spare.** Short declaratives. Cut every word that isn't load-bearing. If a sentence still works with three fewer words, it was three words too long.
- **Functional, not decorative.** Bauhaus logic: the copy states what the thing is and what it does. No metaphor for its own sake.
- **Cold and clean.** Northern, not tropical. Stone, water, breath, iron. Never warm-and-fuzzy, never sweaty-and-loud.
- **Collective, not heroic.** "We," "the crew," "the boat." The unit is the eight, not the individual. No one is the main character.
- **Quietly certain.** State things as fact. Confidence needs no volume — never sell, never oversell, never plead.
- **Effort is respected, not romanticized.** The work is hard and that's simply true. No suffering porn, no motivational bark.

### Do

- One idea per sentence.
- Concrete nouns: stroke, split, rate, water, distance, minute.
- Lowercase body copy, uppercase for eyebrows and labels.
- Periods. Full stops carry the tone.
- Numbers when numbers are the point.

### Don't

- No exclamation points. Ever.
- No Viking/Norse/pirate language: no *conquer*, *Valhalla*, *warrior*, *raid*, *longship*, *saga*, *berserk*.
- No bro register: no *crush*, *beast mode*, *grind*, *savage*, *let's go*.
- No wellness register: no *journey*, *your best self*, *mindset*, *energy*, *transform*, *glow*.
- No questions to the reader. No second-person coaching ("you've got this").
- No em-dash pileups, no three-clause sentences, no ellipses.
