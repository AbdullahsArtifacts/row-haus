The method

A boat only moves
when it moves together.

We train the stroke before we train the number. Every seat in the room is on the same rate, and the room only goes as fast as its slowest catch. That constraint is the whole point.

  1. 01

    Set the rate

    Ten minutes of low-rate work at 18–20 spm. No screens, no split times — just the sequence: legs, body, arms.

  2. 02

    Hold the line

    Intervals built on consistency, not peaks. If your third piece is faster than your first, you started too slow.

  3. 03

    Load the land

    Twelve minutes off the erg. Hinge, pull, carry — the posture that keeps the last five hundred metres honest.

  4. 04

    Come down

    Breath work and thoracic mobility in the dark, with the fjord still running on the wall.
